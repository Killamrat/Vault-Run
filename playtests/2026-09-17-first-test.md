# Playtest 001 — Sept 17, 2026
**Who:** Jake + Cam · **Build:** v0.1.2 grey-box

## Verdict
"Fun and cool but bland and boring."

## Read
The skeleton works — core loop functions, extraction pays out, nothing broken enough to mention. The failure mode is missing drama, not missing systems. "Bland" at grey-box is expected; "not fun" would have been fatal. We passed the fatal check.

## Suspected causes (to verify next session)
1. No escalating tension — floors 1–3 feel the same; multiplier is a number, not visible danger
2. Nothing loud — no detection stings, klaxons, screen shake, or spectacle on failure (GDD Pillar 2)
3. Rooms don't force decisions — grab-and-go loot with no micro-dilemmas
4. Guards are damage sponges, not threats — no fear via telegraph + consequence
5. Downtime — elevator vote is the only interesting decision per floor

## Questions for next playtest
- Did floor 3 feel different from floor 1?
- Were you ever scared of a guard, or just annoyed?
- Did an elevator argument happen? Was it the fun part?
- Was there a single clippable moment in the session? (Key metric)

## Direction (proposed, not ruled)
Add drama, not features: escalation pass, juice pass, room redesign. Consider building the GDD's Alarm/lockdown system — it is the strongest unbuilt drama generator.
